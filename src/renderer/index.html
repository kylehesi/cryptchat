[ SYS ] ~ HOW_TO_USE

01 // CONNECT

  1. You: press GENERATE_PAYLOAD. The OFFER is copied to your clipboard. Send it to your peer through any messenger.
  2. Peer: paste it into REMOTE_PAYLOAD and press EXECUTE_CONNECT. Their ANSWER is copied; they send it back to you.
  3. You: paste the ANSWER into REMOTE_PAYLOAD and press EXECUTE_CONNECT. The link opens and the KERNEL_LOG header shows LINK: ….

Payloads are single-use. For a new link, generate new ones.

Streaming or sharing screenshots? [ ⚙ ] → PRIVACY → HIDE_IP_AND_PAYLOAD hides payloads and IP addresses on screen. Click a hidden payload to copy it.

02 // VERIFY

Read the VERIFY_CODE aloud to each other. Same code: press MATCH. Different code: press MISMATCH. Someone altered the payloads; reconnect over a channel you trust.

03 // TALK & CHAT

Pick your microphone in MIC_SRC and your speakers or headset in AUDIO_OUT. TOGGLE_MIC mutes you, DEAFEN mutes everything. The slider on the peer's card sets their voice volume. Set an alias above the chat, type, press Enter.

04 // SHARE SCREEN

SHARE_SCR → pick a screen or window. While sharing, SWITCH changes the source (e.g. browser → game) without stopping, and STOP ends it. SHARE_SYSTEM_AUDIO sends your PC's sound (your peer never hears their own voice back). TEXT/CODE = sharp, MOTION/GAMES = smooth. Watching: SYS_VOL (top right) sets the shared sound's volume, [FULL] for fullscreen, [ PIP ] for a floating window.

05 // SEND FILES

Press [ + ] next to SEND, or drop files onto the chat. Your peer must ACCEPT each file. It is saved to Downloads\CryptChat (OPEN_FOLDER shows it). No size limit; files go straight to disk over the same encrypted link. Either side can CANCEL.

06 // NOTIFICATIONS

Messages, incoming files, a dropped link and system alerts play a sound, light up what changed until you look, and flash the taskbar button while CryptChat is in the background. Turn them off, set the volume or try each sound in [ ⚙ ] → NOTIFICATIONS.

07 // TROUBLESHOOT

  • [ NAT_CHECK ] (top left) tells you before connecting whether your network needs TURN. Green: direct should work. Yellow: unclear, or TURN needed and set up. Red: set up TURN first.
  • "ICE failed": your networks cannot reach each other directly (e.g. mobile/CGNAT). One of you needs a TURN relay: [ ⚙ ] → NETWORK.
  • HOLD_OPEN ([ ⚙ ] → NETWORK): after you send your ANSWER, you stay reachable for this long (default 2 min). The peer should inject it before then, or the link may need TURN.
  • RELAY_ONLY hides your IP from the peer (needs TURN).
  • "DTLS failed": the payloads were altered or mixed up. Start over.
