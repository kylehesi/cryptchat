[ SYS ] ~ HOW_TO_USE

01 // CONNECT

  1. You: press GENERATE_PAYLOAD. The OFFER is copied to your clipboard. Send it to your peer through any messenger.
  2. Peer: paste it into REMOTE_PAYLOAD and press EXECUTE_CONNECT. Their ANSWER is copied; they send it back to you.
  3. You: paste the ANSWER into REMOTE_PAYLOAD and press EXECUTE_CONNECT. The link opens and the KERNEL_LOG header shows LINK: ….

Payloads are single-use. For a new link, generate new ones.

02 // VERIFY

Read the VERIFY_CODE aloud to each other. Same code: press MATCH. Different code: press MISMATCH. Someone altered the payloads; reconnect over a channel you trust.

03 // TALK & CHAT

Pick your microphone in MIC_SRC. TOGGLE_MIC mutes you, DEAFEN mutes everything. Set an alias above the chat, type, press Enter.

04 // SHARE SCREEN

SHARE_SCR → pick a screen or window. SHARE_SYSTEM_AUDIO sends your PC's sound (your peer never hears their own voice back). TEXT/CODE = sharp, MOTION/GAMES = smooth. Watching: [FULL] for fullscreen, [ PIP ] for a floating window.

05 // TROUBLESHOOT

  • "ICE failed": your networks cannot reach each other directly (e.g. mobile/CGNAT). One of you needs a TURN relay: [ ⚙ ] → NETWORK.
  • RELAY_ONLY hides your IP from the peer (needs TURN).
  • "DTLS failed": the payloads were altered or mixed up. Start over.
